# **aigenius — Make your idea win.**

**aigenius.win** is the *execution layer* of the TriadicFrameworks ecosystem.  
Where **Critique** gives clarity and **Gradient** gives alignment, **aigenius** gives *momentum* — transforming an aligned idea into a deployable, operational, real‑world outcome.

This is the layer where your idea becomes a plan, your plan becomes a system, and your system becomes something that actually runs.

---

## **Purpose**

aigenius exists to:

- take a fully aligned idea from **Gradient**
- apply structural, regime, and topology signals from **RTT/1–RTT/12**
- generate a clean execution blueprint
- produce actionable steps, modules, and artifacts
- maintain coherence across the entire TriadicFrameworks stack

It is the “operator engine” of your ecosystem — the part that *does*.

---

## **Core Concepts**

### **Clarity → Alignment → Execution**
1. **Critique** — capture the idea  
2. **Gradient** — align the idea  
3. **RTT Engines** — analyze the idea  
4. **aigenius** — *execute* the idea  

### **Execution Blueprint**
aigenius produces:

- structured plans  
- module scaffolds  
- operator workflows  
- deployment maps  
- integration paths  
- execution‑ready artifacts  

### **Ecosystem Integration**
aigenius is fully integrated with:

- **RTT/1 — Structural Engine**  
- **RTT/2 — Regime Mapping**  
- **RTT/3 — Topology Engine**  
- **RTT/12 — Unified Synthesis**  
- **Gradient — Alignment Layer**  
- **Critique — Capture Layer**
