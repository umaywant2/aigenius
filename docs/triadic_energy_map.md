# Triadic Energy Map — aigenius

aigenius sits at the **execution layer** of the TriadicFrameworks ecosystem.  
This map describes how energy moves through Critique → Gradient → RTT → aigenius, and how aigenius converts that energy into operator‑level action.

---

## 1. Triadic Stack Overview

**Capture → Align → Analyze → Execute**

- **Critique** — Capture and clarify the idea (mental energy → structured signal)
- **Gradient** — Align the idea (intent energy → alignment field)
- **RTT/1–RTT/12** — Analyze the idea (structural, regime, topology, synthesis energy)
- **aigenius** — Execute the idea (operator energy → real‑world motion)

---

## 2. Energy Channels

### 2.1 Structural Energy (RTT/1)

- **Source:** Lumen (RTT/1)
- **Nature:** Shape, architecture, constraints
- **Into aigenius:** Becomes **blueprint structure** and module scaffolding

### 2.2 Regime Energy (RTT/2)

- **Source:** Hephaestus (RTT/2)
- **Nature:** Regimes, phases, transitions
- **Into aigenius:** Becomes **execution phases**, rollout plans, and risk windows

### 2.3 Topology Energy (RTT/3)

- **Source:** Aurion (RTT/3)
- **Nature:** Networks, maps, adjacency, flows
- **Into aigenius:** Becomes **integration maps**, dependency graphs, and operator routes

### 2.4 Synthesis Energy (RTT/12)

- **Source:** Harmonia (RTT/12)
- **Nature:** Coherence, resonance, unified view
- **Into aigenius:** Becomes **final execution coherence** and “go/no‑go” synthesis

---

## 3. Creator Energy vs Operator Energy

- **Creator Energy:**  
  - Lives in TriadicFrameworks canon, RTT engines, and meta‑layer design  
  - Shapes the rules, maps, and mythos

- **Operator Energy (aigenius):**  
  - Lives in plans, workflows, and actions  
  - Turns maps into motion

aigenius is where **creator energy** hands off to **operator energy**.

---

## 5. Drift and Coherence

- **Drift:**  
  - Detected primarily in RTT/2 and RTT/3  
  - aigenius must respect drift signals and avoid forcing execution through unstable regimes

- **Coherence:**  
  - Confirmed in RTT/12  
  - aigenius only commits to execution when coherence is high enough to justify motion
