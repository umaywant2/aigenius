# **Creator Review Template — TriadicFrameworks**

A creator review is not a critique, alignment pass, or RTT analysis.  
It is a **meta‑layer reflection** performed by the architect of the canon — a structural, narrative, and coherence evaluation from the vantage point of the creator.

This template ensures every idea, module, engine, or artifact receives a consistent, canon‑aligned creator review.
